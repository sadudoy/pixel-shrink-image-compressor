How to Compress Images in Bulk

  1. Drag and Drop: Select multiple JPEG or WebP files (up to 50MB each) and drop them into the upload zone.
  2. Set Dimensions: Choose to keep original dimensions or resize them in bulk using our aspect ratio locker.
  3. Adjust Quality: Use the slider to balance file size and visual quality.
  4. Download All: Click compress and download your optimized images instantly as a zip or sequential files.

Why Use PixelShrink?

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your photos never leave your device. All compression happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. No servers, no data collection.

Lightning Fast

Because there are no upload or download times to a remote server, images are compressed instantly using your device's processing power.

Bulk Processing

Don't waste time doing it one by one. Select dozens of images at once and shrink them all with a single click.
